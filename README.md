# AEIOS Today: compare versions

https://rgafni.github.io/aeios-compare/

Tabs: Mine (copy of the live site, clock-controlled) · James (his mockup; his "now" markers moved to the demo time) · A · Calm · B · Focus · C · Timeline · D · Chat · E · Bubbles · F · Coach · G · Middle.

The **Preview time** bar at the top sets the demo time with one tap: Now, Morning 7:15, School 10:00, Lunch 11:30, After school 3:10, Homework 3:45, Dinner 6:30, Evening 8:30, Bedtime 9:30 (arrows step earlier/later; arrow keys work too). No dragging. Time is kept in the URL hash, e.g. `#a/t=10:00`; `#bubbles/t=15:45` opens Bubbles directly. Installable: "Install app" in the bar (manifest + service worker; install.js + install.css).

All content is the showcase's sample data. No real student data.

**Shared menu** (`shared/drawer.js` + `drawer.css`): the same left drawer in A–E (edge tab, or each variant's own menu button): checklists, calendar, messages, crash courses, brain teaser, playable chess, grades, clubs, goals, journal, settings. All screens work; state saves on this device.

**E · Bubbles** (`variants/e-bubbles/`): five big top-level bubbles (My day, Now, Calendar, Messages, Everything else). My day opens into sub-bubbles (Homework, Schedule, Checklist, What's due, Responsibilities) with an "All" back button; Everything else opens the shared drawer. Tap one and it grows into a full page (one circle scaled with a CSS transform, FLIP for the title), the rest drift aside; Back (or Esc / Android back) shrinks it home. No popups, no external fonts or scripts, honors reduced motion.

**F · Coach** (`coach/`, restyled for a 13-year-old: neutral palette with one accent, abstract monogram, tight radii, compact type, shared `shared/mature.css`): the landing page is the AI coach's latest text, not a dashboard. Time-aware message with quick-reply chips; two plain icon buttons, Talk (voice call) and Text, with the coach's number as a tiny label (fictional (512) 555-0147; the real voice line would run on Bland); Today's plan as a tappable day-block strip tagged with the check-in time; a 5-minute thinking talk with a streak; Noticed voice-note transcripts from a teacher and a parent plus points. Evening is the night wrap-up check-in. In-app copy stays short and plain (no concept explainers). Call and text are simulated. `#coach/t=15:45` opens it directly; standalone it has a Morning / Afternoon / Evening preview toggle. No external fonts or scripts, honors reduced motion.

**G · Middle** (`middle/`): between self-managed and coach-led. Maya builds her own plan (adds blocks, orders them with up/down, sets her own start time and estimates); the coach only reviews and asks, with small Keep / Adjust prompts ("You put 20 min. Last two took ~40.") behind an opt-in Coach review switch. Skill meters (Planning, Estimates, On time) and check-in switches (after-school off, "Night only") show growth and fading support through state. Talk and Text sit in a small secondary row. Evening: she writes her own check-in first, then gets a short coach reply; the plan shows planned vs. actual minutes. Same look as F (`shared/mature.css`). Sample data only.

**H · Coach v3** (`coach3/`, standalone at `/aeios-compare/coach3/`): the coach shows up across the app, not only behind Talk/Text. Home has inline coach cards in the middle (email dates found, estimate check, conflict, overload, big-task breakdown, next check-in, yesterday's review) and coach notes inside the kid's plan. Six tools: Scan (paste or photo to dated task suggestions the kid adds, edits or skips), Break up (coach steps, kid's times, coach questions short ones), Plan my day (kid builds and orders, coach flags estimate, conflict, overload), Check-ins (next nudge, level ladder that gets lighter, Oct 8 vs Oct 22), Stuck (hints not answers, then message a teacher or parent), Reflect (planned vs actual, accuracy, insight). Talk and Text stay. Hash routes (`#/plan`, `#/extract/photo`, `#/checkins/later`, `#/review/week`...) so back works. Phone-first: 17px body, 44px targets. Fictional data only; calls, texts and sends are simulated.
